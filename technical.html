WI-FI MOTION SENSINGSOFTWARE ONLY, LOCAL
Concept & Desktop Application

Sada, sense motion from Wi-Fi signal.

A Python tool that detects motion from the change in Wi-Fi signal strength between a phone hotspot and a laptop. No extra hardware, no accounts, no cloud. Everything stays on your machine.

How it measures
The laptop reads signal strength every ~0.5 s. After a 15-second calibration of the still room, it compares current fluctuation with the calm baseline. Three readings in a row above the threshold means motion.
Zone estimation
You train three zones (near phone, middle, near laptop). Sada stores a fingerprint of each and classifies new motion by the closest match, then measures its own accuracy and says “not reliable” when it cannot tell.
Privacy by design
The server listens on localhost only and rejects requests from other pages. No tracking, no uploads.
