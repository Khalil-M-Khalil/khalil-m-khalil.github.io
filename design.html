Selected Work

Identity, logos & social.

Three projects that show how I work, from a brand's visual identity to the posts it publishes.

01 / VISUAL IDENTITY

Almira

Arabic calligraphic logo and campaign artwork for a Saudi brand, with a deep green and gold palette carried from the logo into the imagery.

  • Role Visual identity design
  • Brief Craft a luxurious Arabic calligraphic mark and visual system tailored for the Saudi market.
  • Outcome Cohesive green-and-gold identity translated across high-impact campaign artwork.
02 / LOGO & BRAND IDENTITY

Logos: WHY Z

Identity for WHY Z, a creative marketing agency: a question mark that resolves into a Z, a deep green palette, a type system, and applications from business cards to stationery.

  • Role Logo and identity design
  • Brief Build an agency brand bridging creative curiosity ("Why") with complete execution ("Z").
  • Outcome A distinctive logo mark resolving ? into Z, backed by a unified stationery and type system.
03 / SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN

Take Car Taxi

A campaign post for a taxi brand, built on its yellow and black identity: a bold headline, a road-sign metaphor for the months ahead, and the logo applied to the vehicle.

  • Role Social media design
  • Brief Design an attention-grabbing social campaign reinforcing a bold yellow-and-black taxi brand.
  • Outcome High-contrast visuals with road-sign metaphors and vehicle branding that boosted recall.
