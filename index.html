About Me

Visual thinker.
Creative builder.

I'm Khalil Khalil, a designer, photographer, content creator, and visual storyteller based in Syria, working with clients across the Arab world and beyond.

My work lives at the intersection of aesthetics and purpose, across visual, written and technical disciplines, always built to feel intentional.

I also study Computer Science and explore cybersecurity, security awareness, and cyber risk, bringing a creative and analytical lens to digital work.

I work fluently in English and Arabic, creating content that feels native across cultures.
