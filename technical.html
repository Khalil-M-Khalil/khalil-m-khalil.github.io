Concept & Windows Application

Nabra, fix the keyboard layout.

A Windows utility I designed to correct text typed with the wrong Arabic or English keyboard layout. Select the text and use a keyboard shortcut to convert it by key position, without translation or AI.

Product idea
A lightweight background app that makes accidental layout switches quick to fix.
Privacy by design
Conversion happens locally. The app does not need internet access and does not upload or store the text.
Interaction design
Customizable hotkeys and Arabic, English or automatic conversion modes.
